Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-23.

FYR Uploader is used only by the operator of the FYR YouTube channel. With the operator's Google authorization it uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload and manage videos on that channel and to read that channel's statistics.

  • Data accessed: the operator's own channel videos, metadata, captions, thumbnails and channel/video analytics.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and analytics summaries are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Use: solely to publish and evaluate the channel's own videos.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

FYR Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
